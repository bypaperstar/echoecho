How Echo works
  1. Say "echo echo" to wake it up.
  2. Talk naturally — it answers out loud, like a conversation.
  3. Slow work is farmed out to background agents; Echo keeps chatting and weaves the results back in when they land.
  4. Say "that's it" (or go quiet for 10 minutes) to end the session.

Talk out loud → it writes, live

Dictate anything — meeting notes, an email, a poem, a plan. A streaming speech model hears you word by word (the gray ghost tail) and a live formatter writes clean, structured text while you keep talking: headings, lists, numbers as figures, your self-corrections applied to what's already on the page.

  • Say “stop.” — the pen halts instantly.
  • Say “scratch that”, “make that a list”, “new paragraph”, “change X to Y”, “heading …” — they're commands, not text.
  • Corrections work mid-flow: “…goes to Marcus — no wait, Marcia.”
